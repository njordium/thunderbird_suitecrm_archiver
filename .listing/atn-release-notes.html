The compose strip no longer goes out with your mail The line in the compose window saying whether a recipient is in SuiteCRM sits inside the message editor, because that is where Thunderbird lets an add-on draw, and so it was sent as the first line of the message: recipients read "name is not in SuiteCRM" above your greeting. It now leaves the message just before sending, and comes back if sending fails. It no longer accepts the cursor either, so nothing you type can end up inside it. The compose strip respects your account choice It appeared in every compose window, including ones sending from accounts you had switched the add-on off for in Preferences. It now follows the same setting as everything else, by the account behind the From line, and changing the From line shows or hides it.
